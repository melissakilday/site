## Conclusions
1. "Near me" within ~15 minutes of Kaukapakapa is the real keyword; it is won by the Google profile (proximity, reviews, categories), not by suburb-name pages.
2. Nanoplasty is the largest term but not offered: remove it from the Google profile; capture the demand honestly with a nanoplasty-vs-keratin article.
3. Helensville is the growth ring and reviews are the lever. Reviews that naturally mention the service and town add relevance.
4. Wainui has no measurable demand: mention only, no page. Kumeū is bigger but distant.
5. Keratin demand peaks Oct–Dec: push the keratin page now.
6. Balayage is the highest-value service but needs page depth, not a title tweak.
