# Thank-you text (send after an appointment)

```
Hi [name], thanks for coming in today. If you're happy with your hair, a quick Google review would really help my small salon: hairbymelissa.co.nz/review Thanks! Melissa
```

- Never offer discounts or freebies in exchange for reviews — this breaks Google's policy and can get reviews removed.
- Send it the same day, while the appointment is still fresh in the client's mind.
